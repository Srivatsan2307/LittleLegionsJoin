Delete your Little Legions account

Little Legions · Silver Spectre Software

You can request deletion of the information stored for your Little Legions account. Deleting the account permanently closes it. This includes the commander name, player tag, roster, collection, gold, gems, cups, battle records, Order membership, and any email saved on the account. In-game currency is not refunded, whether it was earned or bought.

In the game

Open Settings, then Delete account, and confirm. The account is deleted and this phone starts a new roster. Starting a new game without Delete account does not remove the old one.

Without the app

If you have uninstalled Little Legions, email support@silverspectresoftware.com from the address that saved the roster. Say that you want the Little Legions account deleted. We delete the account and the data listed above.

A roster that was never saved to an email can only be deleted from the game, with Settings → Delete account. An email we do not have cannot be matched to that roster.
