Little Legions · Silver Spectre Software

Your commander

You pick a name when you start. It can be 3 to 12 characters. The first change is free. After that, a change costs 50 gems. Your player tag is shown in Settings.

Save progress

Settings → Save progress asks for an email. We send a one-time code. Enter that code and the roster is tied to the email. On another device, use Switch account and the same email.

Battles and the camp

Ranked and Casual use your saved decks. Friendly battles inside an Order do not change cups. Battle logs keep the armies from the start of the fight.

Orders

You can found an Order or join one. A commander can copy an invite link. The person who opens it can join that Order in the app.
