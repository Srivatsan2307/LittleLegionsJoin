For parents

Little Legions · Silver Spectre Software · 23 November 2026

Little Legions is a cartoon strategy game about collecting creatures and fighting with a small army. It is not made for children under 13.

Money

Gold and gems are used inside the game and cannot be cashed out. The shop may also offer purchases for real money through the app store. The store makes that charge, and a parent can allow or block it with the store's purchase approval. The game does not show ads.

What a player can share

A player picks a name. Other players can see that name and the army used in a battle. Private profile, in Settings, hides the profile page from an opponent. Chat is limited to Order requests and friendly-battle posts, not a free-form messenger.

Email

Email is optional. It is used only when a player saves the roster or opens a saved roster on another device. We send a one-time code. We do not send a mailing list.

If you want an account closed

Write to support@silverspectresoftware.com from the email saved on the roster, if there is one, and say that you want it deleted.

The full detail is in the Privacy Policy and the Terms of Service.
